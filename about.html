About

Small on purpose.

Cosine Ventures Ltd is a small lending company, not a call-centre operation. When you ring us, you're talking to someone who can actually make a decision — not a script reading you through a menu.

We're based in Kampala and lend to people we can meet, whose businesses and jobs we can understand — which is part of why we can keep the process short and the terms honest.

Every agreement is explained line by line, in person, before anyone signs. That's the whole approach — nothing more complicated than doing it properly.
