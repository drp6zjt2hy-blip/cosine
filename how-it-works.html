Process

Four steps. No portal to register for.

Everything happens in a conversation, not a queue of email confirmations. Here's exactly what to expect from the moment you reach out.

01

You call or come by

Tell us roughly what you need and why — five or ten minutes is usually enough for us to know if we can help.

02

Bring your documents

National ID, proof of income or trade, and collateral papers if the loan calls for them.

03

We agree the terms

Amount, repayment schedule and cost, walked through in plain language before anything is signed.

04

Funds are sent

Straight to your mobile money or bank account — usually within a day or two of agreeing terms.
