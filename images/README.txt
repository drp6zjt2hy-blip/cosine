HOW TO ADD REAL PHOTOS
=======================

Every page references an image in this folder that doesn't exist yet
(e.g. images/hero.jpg). Until you add the real file, that spot shows
an elegant dark/gold gradient instead of a broken image icon — so the
site never looks broken, just unfinished.

To finish it: drop a photo into this folder with the exact filename
the page expects, and it will appear automatically. No code changes
needed.

Filenames expected, by page:
  index.html          images/hero.jpg
  loans.html          images/loan-salary.jpg
                       images/loan-trader.jpg
                       images/loan-fees.jpg
                       images/loan-asset.jpg
  how-it-works.html   images/how-it-works.jpg
  board.html          images/board-1.jpg  (Managing Director)
                       images/board-2.jpg  (Board Chairperson)
  about.html          images/about.jpg
  contact.html         images/office.jpg

Recommended size: at least 1200px on the long edge, landscape for
hero/section photos, square (1:1) for board portraits.

WHY THESE WEREN'T PULLED FROM THE INTERNET AUTOMATICALLY
----------------------------------------------------------
Every image slot already shows a designed black-and-gold illustration
so the site never looks broken — that's what you see until real
photos are added.

I didn't hotlink real photos of real people found online, even
free-license ones, for two reasons:

  1. Implied endorsement. Most "free" stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels,
     etc.) are licensed for illustration, but using a real, identifiable
     person's photo to represent someone "getting a loan" from a real
     lending business reads as that person endorsing or using your
     product — which most stock licenses don't actually cover, and
     which the person photographed never agreed to.
  2. It's a real, regulated business. A stock photo mismatch (wrong
     context, wrong country, a face someone recognises) is a bigger
     risk for a financial company than for a hobby site.

The safest, most convincing option is still real photos: your own
office, your own clients (with their permission), your own team.
Once you have them, drop them into this folder with the filenames
above and they'll appear automatically — no code changes needed.
